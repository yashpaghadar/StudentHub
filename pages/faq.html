Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I register?

Open the Register page, fill in your details, and click the Register button.

2. How do I log in?

Go to the Login page and enter your registered email and password.

3. How can I update my profile?

After logging in, open the Profile page and edit your information.

4. How do I view upcoming events?

Click on the Events page from the navigation menu to see all upcoming college events.

5. How can I contact the college?

Visit the Contact page and submit the contact form or use the provided contact details.
