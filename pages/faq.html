Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about StudentHub Portal.

General Questions

1. What is StudentHub Portal?

StudentHub is an online portal that helps students access academic information, events, announcements, and profile details.

2. Who can use StudentHub?

Students, faculty members, and administrators can use the StudentHub Portal.

3. How do I register on StudentHub?

Open the Register page, fill in your details, and submit the registration form.

4. How can I log in?

Use your registered email address and password on the Login page.

5. What should I do if I forget my password?

Click the "Forgot Password" link on the Login page and follow the instructions.

6. Can I update my profile?

Yes. After logging in, open the Profile page and update your personal information.

7. How can I register for college events?

Visit the Events page, select your preferred event, and complete the registration form.

8. How do I submit feedback?

Open the Feedback page, fill out the feedback form, and click the Submit button.

9. How do I contact the college?

You can visit the Contact page to find the college email address, phone number, and contact form.

10. Where can I see the latest announcements?

Latest announcements are available on the Home page and Dashboard.
