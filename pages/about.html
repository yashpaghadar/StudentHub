About StudentHub

StudentHub is a web-based portal designed to help students, faculty members, and administrators manage campus activities efficiently. It provides a single platform for accessing academic information, college events, announcements, and student services.

The portal improves communication within the institution and helps students stay informed about the latest campus activities and important updates.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a simple, reliable, and user-friendly portal that supports students in their academic journey by providing quick access to important information and services.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a smart digital campus where every student can easily access academic resources, participate in events, and communicate with the college through a single online platform.

Objectives

  • Provide a centralized portal for students.
  • Share college announcements and notices.
  • Manage campus events and activities.
  • Maintain student profile information.
  • Improve communication between students and faculty.
  • Collect student feedback for better services.

Features of StudentHub

Student Dashboard

Students can view personal information, announcements, and upcoming events after logging into the portal.

Event Management

Students can check upcoming workshops, seminars, coding competitions, and cultural events organized by the college.

Profile Management

Students can view and update their personal profile details whenever required.

Feedback System

Students can submit suggestions and feedback to help improve campus facilities and services.
